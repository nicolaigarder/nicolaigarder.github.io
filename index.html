Projects

Forecasting Inflow to Bygholm Lake for Flood Prevention

Bachelor thesis · DTU · 2025

Flood risk in Horsens is rising with climate change, and the municipality regulates Bygholm Lake through a sluice gate. Using historical weather data from DMI and daily flow data from Vandportalen, I built and compared models that forecast daily inflow to the lake: a persistence baseline, an ARX model updated online with Recursive Least Squares, and an LSTM neural network in TensorFlow.

The LSTM was most accurate overall, but the adaptive ARX/RLS model was more stable during extreme inflow events — the ones that matter most for flood prevention. Rainfall duration was the strongest predictor in both models, followed by radiation, minimum temperature and wind speed (key drivers of evapotranspiration).
