Projects

Bachelor thesis · DTU · 2025

Forecasting Inflow to Bygholm Lake for Flood Prevention

Flood risk in Horsens is rising with climate change, and the municipality regulates Bygholm Lake through a sluice gate. Using weather data from DMI and daily flow data from Vandportalen, I built and compared models that forecast daily inflow to the lake: a persistence baseline, an ARX model updated online with Recursive Least Squares, and an LSTM neural network.

LSTMMost accurate across the full test set
ARX / RLSMost stable during extreme inflow events

Rainfall duration was the strongest predictor in both models, followed by radiation, minimum temperature and wind speed — key drivers of evapotranspiration.
